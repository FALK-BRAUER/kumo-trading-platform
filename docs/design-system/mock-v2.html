Commit is a two-phase saga, not one atomic order — the card shows the real broker state, never the intended one:
① fill · ADD 12 / 20 filled @ 259 · stop realign pending
② protected · net 60 · one stop @ 250.30 · aligned ✓
⚠ stop replace REJECTED · 20 sh uncovered · old stop re-armed for 40 · [Repair — protect now] · algos killed
Trim/scale-out shrinks the net stop qty to the remainder (keep price = default · option → breakeven; not the add's risk-grow choice) + realizes P&L. Stop = trail/BE/ATR/manual on the one resting order (shows broker order-id + status). Target = the OCO leg of the protection set. Flatten = the card's quick slide. Engine drives cancel-replace fill-driven; a reject re-arms the old stop or forces Repair — protection is never assumed, always reconciled to the broker.
